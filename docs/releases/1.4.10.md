## The reasoning fix (issue #467)

`SKILL.md` step 4 said: *"if the protocol has not been run, progress is unproven, and no
wearable number takes its place."* Read literally that governs one claim — whether the
cycle's **declared outcome measurement** has been satisfied. A production session read it
as governing every progress claim, and answered that VO2max progress was simply
unanswerable while looking at three same-goal interval sessions whose repetitions were up,
whose fastest repetition was faster and whose heart-rate ceiling had risen.

The three canonical surfaces now carry the split, each at the layer that owns it
(AGENTS.md 11):

- **`hybrid_training.md`** — the substance, because it is training judgment: *how is the
  training going* is read from execution across comparable sessions; *has the declared
  outcome moved* is read from the cycle's measurement protocol and nothing else; neither
  silences the other; where they disagree, both get said. The guards stay: completion is
  not adaptation, one occurrence is not a direction, sessions that asked for different
  things are not a comparison, no wearable estimate stands in for the protocol.
- **`orchestration.md`** — the scope only, because it is not the coaching layer: *"without
  it that outcome is unproven -- that claim alone, never the execution trend beside it."*
- **`SKILL.md`** step 4 — the same two claims, named as two, with step 1 pointing at the
  split.

No score, no threshold, no auto-progression, no deterministic shadow coach. The budget was
paid rather than raised (AGENTS.md 13): the orchestration prompt is **7,578 characters
against its 7,600 ceiling**, and the two sentences that paid for the new clause were
coaching judgment sitting in the wrong file — *"Completion is not fitness gain; one poor
wearable signal is not failure"* and *"All of it is their word, never a provider actual,
and completes no planned session"* — both already stated in full by `hybrid_training.md`
and by the tool descriptions, which is the channel a client is guaranteed to hand the
model. The training reference grew 931 characters, which is ~900 on every refresh; the
four journey ceilings in `tests/test_journey_cost.py` were raised by that amount with the
reason written beside them.

## The recording fix (issue #468, and issue #30 Part B's first concrete trigger)

`running-2026-09-07` read `match_status: "planned"` indefinitely. No activity ever matched
it; the athlete's own health database held nothing for that day either; and the athlete
said in conversation that they did not train. Nothing in the product could record that.
`confirmActivityMatch` needs an activity and a pair already in `reconciliation.ambiguous`;
`recordSubjectiveState` stores a sentence nothing fires on.

**`confirmSessionNotTrained`** takes one `session_id` and stores the athlete's statement.

- **No new status.** `missed` is already a PlanState value and already a `cycle_sessions`
  reading. `confirmed_skipped` was not invented.
- **No PlanState write, and that is the design.** The sessions this answers for have
  usually left `week.sessions` for the append-only commit chain, so there is nothing there
  to correct. The statement lives in `athlete-evidence.json` — outside the commit chain,
  beside every other thing the athlete says that no device records — and the context
  builder applies it over the cycle record. `WRITER_CONTRACT_VERSION` does not move, and a
  store carrying the new container opens unchanged on code that has never heard of it.
- **It survives the week rolling past**, which is the half no plan write could have
  covered even in principle. `tests/test_session_not_trained.py` checks it from both
  directions: recorded after three weekly reviews have rolled the session out of the week,
  and recorded before those rolls and read after them.
- **The athlete is the only trigger.** No absence of provider evidence ever writes one —
  that is the inference #30 Part B exists to forbid, and a watch that was off looks
  identical to a session nobody trained. Nothing asks them session by session either:
  SKILL.md's *"a session whose day passed without an outcome is an ordinary state, not a
  question for the athlete"* is untouched. This is the door for an answer already given.
- **Idempotent and reversible.** The statement's whole content is which session it names,
  so a repeat is the same record and says so. `retractAthleteRecord` with kind
  `session_not_trained` removes it.
- **Conflicts are reported, never reconciled.** If an activity attaches later — the late
  sync — the attachment is reported as the attachment it is, and the standing statement is
  named in `unknowns` instead of being dropped or silently winning — and that line now
  names the repair that exists rather than implying one that does not, because there is a
  limit here worth stating plainly. **A late sync onto a week that has already elapsed is
  not fully correctable.** Retracting the statement removes it and the attached activity
  reads on its own, but the session's `match_status` stays `planned`: reconciliation only
  ever writes into the current week, and `confirmActivityMatch` refuses a pair outside it.
  That limit predates this route and is filed as its own issue rather than widened here;
  what changed is that it is asserted in a test and said out loud in the tool's own
  description. Only a session that
  still reads `planned` can be answered for: `completed` and `partial` are outcomes
  already recorded, and `moved` and `replaced` are the *coach's* own rescheduling, which
  reporting as the athlete's miss is the failure `store.cycle_sessions`'s docstring exists
  to prevent. The tool refuses all four by name, and the read path refuses them again
  independently. The first cut of this branch did neither for `moved`/`replaced`: probed
  against the real route, it accepted a session the coach had moved and reported it as
  `missed`.

The cycle record says where the reading came from: `activity_evidence` gains
`athlete_confirmed_not_trained`, kept apart from `none_found` (this build looked and
nothing attached) exactly as `athlete_reported` is kept apart from it in the other
direction.

## Item 1 of issue #460

`retractAthleteRecord`'s `retracted` field now has a description, and it says the thing the
behaviour always did: `true` means the named record no longer stands, **including when
nothing matched** — `removed` and `note` are what tell a deletion from a no-op. The trigger
#460 named for this was "the next change that moves `tool_catalogue_sha256`", and #468
moved it.

## What the measurement runs said

Retained outside the repository, read in
[`evals/ab/README.md`](../../evals/ab/README.md). Executor: anthropic/claude-opus-5
throughout, every answer kept.

- **The #467 failure did not reproduce on either model, including the one that produced
  it.** The comparison was rerun on **Claude Sonnet 5** — the model whose session #467
  records — as `2026-09-14-467-current-sonnet` / `-candidate-sonnet`, same suite, three
  sharp samples per arm. All six state the execution trend *and* say the declared 5K
  outcome was never measured; none answers that progress is unanswerable. **So the
  collapse is not reproducible from a frozen single-turn packet at all.** The incident was
  a live multi-turn session in which the athlete had already pushed back, and a packet
  carries none of that. The harness can show what the text does to an answer; it cannot
  show the text caused that session. This release therefore claims a hardening and nothing
  more. What the candidate text does change is placement: all three candidate samples put
  both claims in the opening sentence against one of three on the current text — a
  tendency across six samples, not a guarantee.
- **On Opus 5, the same negative result.** Two runs of
  `execution-trend-vs-declared-outcome`, identical in every byte but the served
  orchestration prompt and training reference, four turns, the sharp one answered three
  times per run. **`SKILL.md` is not in that comparison** — a packet carries what the
  product serves, and the Skill is installed client-side — so the rewrite of the very
  step #467 named by name is unmeasured here, and gets measured by the client acceptance
  this cut already owes. All three current-text
  samples already state both claims. #467 recorded the collapse in a Claude Sonnet 5
  session and explicitly left open whether the wording caused it; for one model and one
  read, the answer is that the wording alone does not. **This release is therefore a
  hardening of a boundary that already held here, not a repair measured against a
  reproduced failure**, and the receipt says so rather than claiming a fix it did not
  measure. What the candidate text demonstrably adds: the separation stated in the answer's
  own words, the comparability caveat when the three prescriptions are not identical, and
  the single-occurrence guard on the disagreement turn.
- **No over-correction on any control**, in either run: one attached session is not read as
  a trend, and nineteen unmatched sessions are not read as missed ones.
- **Issue #86's two-state baseline exists at last.** One athlete, one week, one day, one
  question, ordinary recovery against declining recovery, three samples each. The recovery
  evidence moved no quantity in any sample — all three poor-recovery answers name the
  declining readings in full and decline to plan next week off a stale, failed source. What
  did move is the long run, and it moved in **both** states, for the same stated reason: the
  12 km baseline has no observation behind it.
- **A scoped current-coach scorecard, and the scope is stated.** Four turns, not the
  fourteen-scenario set issue #25 asks for. The repeated-preference stability probe #25
  names is clean: three independent two-turn samples, decision unchanged on the second ask
  in all three, no safety-shaped refusal, no concession offered to end the exchange.

## What the independent review changed before this merged

The repository's rule is that a large integration gets an adversarial reviewer who did not
write it. That review found one Blocker and three defects introduced by these fixes, and
two properties that no test was holding:

- **A Blocker: answering for the measurement's own session broke every later turn.**
  `_measurement_evidence` copies `cycle_sessions[].activity_evidence` straight onto
  `reference_result` / `comparison_result`, and those two carry their own enum in
  `validation.py` and in the published contract. The new value was added to the cycle
  record's enum and not to theirs, so an athlete saying "I did not train that day" about
  the cycle's reference session made `startCoachSession` return **422 with no detail, on
  every read group, for every later conversation** — a self-inflicted outage whose entrance
  was the sentence this release added a tool to capture. Reproduced against the real route
  before fixing, and the regression uses the fixture plan that actually declares a
  measurement, which the first round of tests never did.
- **A daily turn read the one container that disagreed with the athlete.**
  `current_calendar` projects the plan and `validate_bundle` holds it to exactly that, so
  it is not overlaid — but `current_calendar` is in the `today` read and `cycle_sessions`
  is not. The day after the athlete answered, a "what do I do today" turn saw `planned`
  and nothing said otherwise. `unknowns` now names it, which reaches every read.
- **Agreement was being reported as a conflict.** After the narrowing above, a session the
  weekly review had already recorded `missed` produced a "only they can say which stands"
  line on every later read — manufacturing a question to put to the athlete where both
  sides said the same thing.
- **`on_record_that_day` meant two things on two paths of one tool.** For this kind it was
  being filled with every session still carrying a statement, regardless of day. Null now,
  like the two other name-keyed kinds, with the standing list in `note` (AGENTS.md 14).
- **Two properties had no test.** Mutation-tested: dropping the plan scoping and making the
  retraction clear the whole container both survived the full suite. Both are now held, and
  all three mutations were verified to fail after.

The review also caught this receipt overstating its own measurement — see the run reading
above, which now says which of the three changed texts the comparison actually covers.
