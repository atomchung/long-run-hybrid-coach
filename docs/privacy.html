Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 August 2026 · Operated by an independent developer in Taiwan.

This policy explains what Long Run Hybrid Coach processes, why it is needed, where it goes, and how to revoke access or request deletion.

1. Data processed and purpose

DataPurpose
Completed activitiesReconcile planned training against what you actually completed and maintain the current direction.
Available wellness summariesInform coaching judgment when the evidence is present and current. Missing or stale fields remain unknown.
Planned calendar workoutsRead the current calendar, prevent duplicate ownership conflicts, and publish only workouts you approved.
Goals, constraints, and preferencesMake the plan usable for your schedule and stated direction.
OAuth authorizationAccess your Intervals.icu account within the scopes you grant. Your Intervals.icu password is never collected.

2. Storage and authorization

The hosted gateway stores owner-scoped training state, including plans, decisions, approvals, and observable delivery receipts. Provider identity and a keyed token fingerprint are stored separately so requests can be resolved to the same owner.

The raw Intervals.icu OAuth access token is used for the authorized request but is not written to the state store or application logs. The OAuth client secret stays server-side and is not exposed to the GPT.

3. AI processing

Coaching judgment is produced in ChatGPT under the account through which you use the product. The training context required for the response is therefore processed by OpenAI under its applicable terms and privacy policy. The Long Run Hybrid Coach gateway does not call a separate large-language-model API or hold an OpenAI API key on your behalf.

If you do not want training or wellness information processed by ChatGPT, do not use the product.

4. Connected platform

Long Run Hybrid Coach reads from and writes to Intervals.icu under the authorization scopes you grant. Intervals.icu handles that data under its own terms and privacy policy. The product does not sell training data, use it for advertising, or write completed activities or wellness records.

5. Retention and deletion

Owner-scoped training state is retained while you use the product so that current decisions can be reconciled and audited. You may request deletion of the active state and identity mapping. Residual copies in routine operational backups, if any, expire through the applicable backup rotation.

To stop future access immediately, revoke Long Run Hybrid Coach in Intervals.icu. Revocation prevents future authorized reads and writes. You can remove product-created workouts from your Intervals.icu calendar.

6. Security

Authorization uses OAuth. Raw access tokens are not persisted or logged; account lookup uses a keyed HMAC-SHA256 fingerprint. Owner state is separated by owner identifier, credentials are not committed to source control, and private training records do not belong in this public website repository.

7. Your choices and rights

You may request access, correction, export, or deletion of hosted data associated with your owner account. You may also revoke Intervals.icu access at any time through Intervals.icu.

8. Children

Long Run Hybrid Coach is not intended for anyone under 16 and is not directed at children.

9. Not medical advice

Long Run Hybrid Coach is a training-planning tool. It does not diagnose, treat, or monitor medical conditions and is not a substitute for a physician or qualified human coach.

10. Contact

Contact the developer through the public repository issue tracker. Do not include health data, credentials, OAuth tokens, or other private information in a public issue; request a private follow-up channel instead.

11. Changes

If this policy changes materially, the updated date above will change and the new version will be published at this URL.
